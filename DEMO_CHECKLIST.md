# Demo checklist (Working Application)
1. Open the live site on a phone; sign up and log in.
2. Add 2 emergency contacts (use your own e-mail for one).
3. Dashboard -> turn monitoring ON, allow location + motion permissions; show speed and risk bar.
4. Press SOS -> show confirmation, then the e-mail with Google Maps link.
5. Trigger a firm knock (>2.8 g) -> 15 s countdown -> press "I'm OK - Cancel Alert" -> history shows Cancelled.
6. Trigger again and let it reach zero -> alert Sent, contacts e-mailed.
7. Show Alert History and the Driving Behaviour chart.
Backup: keep screenshots/screen recording of steps 3-6 in case GPS or e-mail fails during the demo.
